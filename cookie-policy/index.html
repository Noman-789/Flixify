COOKIE POLICY

Learn how we use cookies and similar technologies on our website to improve your experience and optimize our services.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit our website. They help us remember your preferences, understand how you use our site, and improve your overall experience.

Cookies help us remember who you are, what you prefer, and what you've done before — so every visit is smoother and more relevant to you.

Types of Cookies We Use

Type Purpose Duration
Strictly Necessary Essential for the website to function properly Session
Performance Track traffic and usage analytics 1–2 Years
Functionality Remember preferences and settings 1 Year
Targeting / Ads Deliver relevant advertisements 1–2 Years
Third Party Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, etc. Varies

Cookies We Specifically Use

Cookie Type Description
session_id Necessary Identifies your session and maintains basic website functionality
_ga _ga_* Performance Google Analytics — analyzes page views, bounce rate, user flow
_fbp Targeting Meta Pixel — measures ad performance and enables retargeting
cookie_consent Necessary Stores your cookie consent preferences
preferred_lang Functionality Remembers your selected language preference

How to Manage Cookies

You can block, delete, or manage cookies through your browser settings. Note that blocking certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

Google Chrome

Settings → Privacy → Cookies

Mozilla Firefox

Settings → Privacy → Cookies

Apple Safari

Preferences → Privacy → Cookies

Microsoft Edge

Settings → Cookies → Manage

Your Rights

You have full control over your cookie data. Here's what you can do:

Access & View

Request to see what cookie data we have stored about you.

Delete

Request deletion of your cookie data at any time.

Opt-Out

Reject non-essential cookies through our consent banner or browser settings.

Data Portability

Request your data in a machine-readable format.
