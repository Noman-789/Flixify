QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a project?
Fill out our contact form or message us on WhatsApp. We'll schedule a free consultation to discuss your requirements and provide a detailed proposal.
Do you offer post-launch support?
Yes! We offer ongoing support packages covering bug fixes, minor updates, and performance monitoring. Details are included in every project proposal.
What if I need changes after delivery?
Each project includes defined revision rounds. Additional changes beyond scope can be accommodated at an agreed hourly rate. We're always flexible.
Can I migrate from another platform?
Absolutely. We handle migrations from WooCommerce, Magento, Wix, and custom platforms — including products, customers, orders, and SEO data.
Do you work with international clients?
Yes, we work with clients globally. Our communication is primarily through WhatsApp, email, and video calls, which makes geography irrelevant.
